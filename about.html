Our Story

Crafted with Pride,
Worn with Love

ShionHouse was founded in 2018 in Mumbai by Priya and Arjun Sharma — two fashion designers who grew up watching their grandmothers drape handloom sarees and watching weavers work their magic on backstrap looms in rural Varanasi.

Frustrated that these extraordinary skills were being lost to fast fashion, they built ShionHouse as a bridge — a place where handcrafted Indian garments could find a new, appreciative audience without losing their soul. Every product on our platform is sourced directly from verified artisans, with fair wages guaranteed.

"India has the most extraordinary textile tradition in the world. We want every Indian to wear that pride."

— Priya Sharma, Co-Founder

Today ShionHouse partners with over 350 artisans across 22 Indian states — from Kashmiri Pashmina weavers to Rajasthani block printers, Banarasi silk weavers to Kolhapuri leather craftspeople.

The Hands Behind Every Piece

Artisan Spotlight

🧵 Varanasi, UP
Silk Weaving
Ramesh Prasad Gupta

A third-generation Banarasi weaver who learned the craft from his father at age 12. Ramesh and his team create our signature zari-work silk sarees on traditional handlooms — a process that takes 3 to 7 days per saree.

🎨 Jaipur, Rajasthan
Block Printing
Sunita Devi

Sunita runs a 14-woman cooperative in Sanganer, Jaipur — famous worldwide for its hand-block printed textiles. Using vegetable dyes and hand-carved wooden blocks, her team brings our cotton collection to life with motifs passed down over centuries.

👡 Kolhapur, Maharashtra
Leather Craft
Vijay Kamble

Vijay is a master Kolhapuri chappal maker whose family has been hand-stitching leather footwear for over 60 years. Each pair is hand-cut and stitched using only locally sourced leather, producing footwear that improves with every wear.
